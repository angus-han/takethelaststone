🪨 Take The Last Stone 🪨

Gameplay

This game involves two players and a pile of stones.

The players will take turns to remove stones from the pile. The player who removes the last stone from the pile wins the game.

The players must agree on the maximum number of stones that a player can remove on each turn. So on each turn, a player must remove at least one stone, and may remove at most that maximum number.
